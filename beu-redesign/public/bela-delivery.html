🛵Bela Delivery

Food, groceries and parcels across Addis, in a few taps.

A delivery app concept in warm orange that works end to end. Browse, customise dishes, check out, then watch your rider cross the city on a live map.

  1. Pick a restaurant and add a dish. Try the spice and extras options.
  2. In your cart, apply the code WELCOME15 and choose how to pay.
  3. Place the order and follow the rider. It arrives in about a minute.
  4. Rate it, reorder it from Orders, or send a parcel with Courier.

Bela is a fictional brand made for this demo. Orders are simulated and nothing is charged.
