The premise

Since 2002, the woman in the oval has welcomed every guest who has arrived. Five places, one quiet promise: stone that holds the day’s heat, cotton that holds the light, and water always within sight.

Dine

Buna.
Three rounds, never hurried.

Coffee was found in these highlands. At Kuriftu it is still roasted by hand over coals, ground in a mortar and poured from the jebena in three rounds, each one a reason to stay a little longer.

  1. i.

    Abol

    The first pour. Strongest, darkest, shared as a welcome.

  2. ii.

    Tona

    The second. Gentler, for conversation.

  3. iii.

    Baraka

    The third, the blessing. The one that sends you on your way.
