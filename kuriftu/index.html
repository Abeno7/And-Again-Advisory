The premise

Kuriftu has welcomed guests since 2002. The woman in the oval has welcomed every one of them.

Five places across Ethiopia, held together by one welcome. A crater lake at Bishoftu. A eucalyptus forest above Addis. The river gorge at Awash. The papyrus shores of Lake Tana. A village built as a map of a continent.

Each is quieter than you expect. Stone that holds the day's heat, cotton that holds the light, and water always within sight.

Discover the five places

Destinations

Five places. The name is enough.

Guests choose Kuriftu first, then the place. Each destination keeps its own character in its light, its materials and its pace.

  1. 01 Bishoftu Crater lake · 45 minutes from Addis Ababa →

    The house on the crater lake. Stone villas, lakeside dining and the jetty at sundown.

  2. 02 Entoto Eucalyptus highlands · Above Addis Ababa →

    Raised tents in the tall eucalyptus. Cool air, woodsmoke, and the city far below.

  3. 03 Awash Falls River gorge · Awash National Park →

    Lodges on the lip of the gorge, where the river falls and the plain goes gold at dusk.

  4. 04 Lake Tana Lakeshore · Bahir Dar →

    Morning mist on Ethiopia's great lake, island monasteries and the source of the Blue Nile.

  5. 05 African Village Villas of a continent · Burayu →

    Each villa drawn from an African nation: its architecture, its craft and its colour.

I · The table

The coffee ceremony, three rounds slow.

Beans roasted at the table, ground by hand and poured from the jebena: abol, tona, baraka. Our kitchens cook the same way, with fire, clay and the produce of the valley.

Reserve a table

II · Spa & rituals

Warm stone, still water.

Treatments built on Ethiopian traditions of oil, herb and heat. Steam rooms of basalt, baths open to the sky, and therapists who are never in a hurry.

  • Hot-stone and herbal oil rituals
  • Open-air baths and steam
  • Yoga at first light, by the water
Book a treatment

III · Stay

Rooms that look better in their tenth year.

Villas, lodges and raised forest tents in local stone, eucalyptus and handwoven cotton. Chosen for how they age: basalt darkens, brass warms, cotton softens.

The world of Kuriftu

Eight materials, nothing borrowed.

Every material already belongs to the places Kuriftu stands on. None needs importing to feel luxurious.

  • BasaltThe volcanic ground of the Rift.

  • Eucalyptus timberEntoto's forest, warm and oiled.

  • Handwoven cottonShemma cloth, from netela to bed linen.

  • BrassThe gold of the mark, made physical.

  • Fired clayThe jebena, the pot, the earthen wall.

  • Grass weaveThe mesob's geometry at room scale.

  • Raw linenSofter every year it is used.

  • Still waterBishoftu, Tana, the falls at Awash.
