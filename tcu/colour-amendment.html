The five steps are not interchangeable. Each one has a job. Terracotta stays the signature and is reserved for the name bar and endorsement panel; Lime Wash carries the page; Deep Walnut takes the covers and footers. Used in this order the material reads as Treesongs before a single word is read.

Rules for application

Ground first

Lime Wash or TCU paper carries roughly half of any piece. Reach for Deep Walnut only when the format asks for drama.

One accent

Terracotta appears once or twice per spread. If it starts to look like a brand colour scheme, remove some.

Clay is a ground, not a text colour

At 1.86:1 against paper it will not hold body copy. Use it behind type, never as type.

Never

No Riversprings green or Whisperlake grey on Treesongs material. No gradients between steps.

Rules for application & the reference palette

Let the two ends breathe

Spring Light and Deep Forest do the heavy lifting. River Green is punctuation between them, not a wash.

Meadow is a ground

At 1.80:1 on paper it cannot hold text. It fills, tints and scrims — Fern Shadow takes over for type.

WHISPERLAKE

Palette in application
12

Whisperlake is the quietest of the three and its palette is deliberately narrow. Almost everything happens between Dawn and Vapour, with Deep Slate reserved for type and covers. Mist is a field colour only — it is too light to carry text and must never be asked to.

Serene

Digital & interface

Deep Slate — not the core — takes buttons and links, because Mist cannot meet contrast.

Rules for application

Mist is a field, never type

The core reaches 1.98:1 on paper. It fills bars, panels and rules. Deep Slate carries every word.

Keep the range narrow

Dawn to Vapour is the working range. The jump to Deep Slate should feel like an event, not a habit.

Protect the white space

Whisperlake pages carry less ink than the other two. Resist filling the ground.

Never

No warm greys, no Treesongs clay, no emboldening of the lowercase name artwork.

AA — ratio 4.5:1 or better. Cleared for body copy, captions, labels and interface text at any size.

Large only — ratio between 3:1 and 4.5:1. Permitted at 24 pt regular or 19 pt bold and above. Never for body copy.

Ground only — below 3:1. These steps exist to fill, tint and scrim. They must never carry text of any size.

Ratios calculated to WCAG 2.2 against TCU paper #F5F1EE and each offspring's own paper step. Print applications should also be proofed on the specified stock.
