Project
Brief

Ethiopia is globally recognized as the birthplace of Coffee Arabica, yet its influence extends far beyond cultivation. It is the origin of centuries of knowledge, tradition, ritual and cultural exchange that have shaped coffee's journey across the world.

Coffee Village is envisioned as a destination that celebrates this legacy while positioning Ethiopia at the forefront of coffee's future. The project brings together culture, research, innovation, tourism and community within a single immersive environment.

Rather than functioning as a conventional museum, the

development is conceived as a living landscape where visitors experience the story of coffee through architecture, nature and curated moments of discovery. Every space contributes to a continuous narrative—from the forests where coffee first emerged to the global conversations that continue to shape its future.

The vision is to establish Coffee Village as an international landmark that reflects Ethiopia's enduring leadership in coffee culture while creating new opportunities for education, innovation and sustainable development.
