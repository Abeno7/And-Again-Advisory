◆ Our Story

Born in Addis.
Built for the World.

Founded in 2008 by Surafel Meressa under the name Ethiopian Ushers and Events (EUE), Spectacle Events has evolved into Ethiopia's premier full-service, integrated event management company — built on the belief that every event deserves flawless execution.

From high-profile African Union summits and diplomatic state visits to corporate milestones and product launches, our team of dedicated professionals delivers strategic, creative, and seamless event experiences. We take the stress out of planning so you can focus on your vision.

How We Work

Our Process

A proven four-step methodology ensuring every event is delivered flawlessly.

01

Strategy & Concept

Define goals, identify target audience, align event format with your brand — content, theme, speaker & exhibitor selection all handled.

02

Planning & Logistics

Budget planning, venue management, vendor coordination — catering, printing, travel, hotel, ticketing — all negotiated and confirmed.

03

Live Execution

On-site AV, interpretation, ushering, speaker coordination, health & safety — every detail managed from setup to final curtain.

04

Post-Event Wrap-Up

Event reporting, media delivery, debriefing sessions, attendee feedback — and organized VIP departure coordination.
